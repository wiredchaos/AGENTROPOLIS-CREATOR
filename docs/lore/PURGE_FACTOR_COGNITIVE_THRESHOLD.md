# PURGE FACTOR — COGNITIVE THRESHOLD PROTOCOL

Status: AUTHOR_DECLARED_CANON
Author declaration date: 2026-08-19
Scope: fictional story-world doctrine

## Core rule

The Purge Factor is based on cognitive power, not race, ethnicity, nationality, sex, religion, wealth, occupation, ancestry, celebrity, political connection, or social class.

The regime establishes a Cognitive Threshold of 140.

- 140 and above: PROTECTED COGNITIVE CLASS
- Below 140: SUBJECT TO PURGE

Within the fiction, the threshold is the regime's classification doctrine. It is not presented as a real-world claim that IQ determines human worth or fully measures cognition.

## Revenge of the Nerds

The historical/cultural name for the reversal is **REVENGE OF THE NERDS**.

The old hierarchy loses its exemptions. Wealth, pedigree, fame, political authority, physical dominance, and inherited status cannot by themselves protect a person. The new order claims that brain power alone determines protection.

This creates the regime's central contradiction: it claims to have eliminated traditional prejudice while creating a new hierarchy based on measured cognition.

## Cognitive Index

The regime may describe its Cognitive Index as measuring multiple dimensions rather than a conventional IQ test alone, including:

- abstract reasoning
- pattern recognition
- strategic prediction
- memory
- systems thinking
- adaptability
- creative synthesis
- resistance to manipulation

The canonical threshold remains **140**.

## Dramatic questions

The story should preserve uncertainty around the measurement system:

- Who designed the Cognitive Index?
- Why was 140 selected?
- Who controls the testing infrastructure?
- Can scores be manipulated, trained, forged, suppressed, or weaponized?
- What forms of intelligence does the system fail to recognize?
- Can a highly capable person intentionally fail?
- How does the system classify artificial or nonstandard cognition?
- Is the threshold merely measuring cognition, or selecting for something deeper?

The governing thematic question is:

**WHO BUILT THE TEST?**
