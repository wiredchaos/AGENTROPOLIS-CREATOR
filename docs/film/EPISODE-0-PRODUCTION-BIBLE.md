# COINTELPRO Episode 0 Production Bible

**Working title:** `THE FIRST ANOMALOUS TRANSMISSION`
**Target runtime:** 72 seconds
**Production state:** existing-media matching plan; no media generation authorized
**Hosted-generation credit ceiling:** `0`

## Story mandate

Episode 0 opens in Neuro Tokyo 2090. Grinder runners move a sealed data reliquary through a rain-soaked port carrying black-market neural freight. A file marked `COINTELPRO` and an unrecognized symbol appear inside. ATVNETWORK hijacks nearby screens and a woman says exactly:

> THIS TRANSMISSION WAS RECORDED 508 YEARS AGO.

The transmission exposes fragments connecting the Yasuke lineage, Chrysantheum, and Grinder without resolving their contested chronology. NETERU and 589 appear only as an unexplained signal tease. The film cuts to black on the exact locked copy `DIRECTIVE AVAILABLE.`

All facts in this paragraph are `AUTHOR_DECLARED_CANON` for the Episode 0 film architecture except the cross-property connections, which are isolated as approved `METAVERSE_SYNTHESIS`. This does not relabel them as source-repository canon.

## Visual lanes

### 789 Studios

Immutable cel-shaded anime. No photoreal skin or surface language may bleed into the lane. Episode 0 does not require a 789 shot merely to advertise the lane; any later use requires approved character and style evidence.

### NTRU Studios

Immutable hyper-real cinematic photorealism with physically based materials, motivated practical lighting, real optical behavior, and weighted physics. Episode 0 does not require an NTRU shot unless its story function is approved.

### Grinder / Neuro Tokyo 2090

An isolated, source-derived cyberpunk lane. The current author declaration establishes rain, port cranes, black-market neural freight, Grinder runners, a sealed data reliquary, and hijacked screens—but not palette, wardrobe, architecture, typography, runner identity, vehicle design, symbol geometry, or rendering technique.

Until authoritative source frames and rights records arrive:

- its working `STUDIO_MODE` is `GRINDER_NEURO_TOKYO_2090`;
- its `STYLE_LOCK` is `SOURCE_DERIVATION_REQUIRED`;
- blocking and camera previs may be planned abstractly;
- no hero-frame or motion generation may execute.

### Civilization reference

`ALKebulan` / `ALKEBULAN` remains canonical. The prohibited comparison must never appear in prompts, filenames, metadata, production design, marketing, or generated assets.

## Sound lock

- Rain, port machinery, freight movement, footfall, enclosure resonance, screen interruption, signal discontinuity, the locked voice line, and final silence each perform distinct narrative functions.
- Music is optional until its source identity is approved; generic cyberpunk synth scoring is prohibited as a default.
- Silence begins immediately after the voice line and returns under the final directive.
- No voice synthesis, cloning, or casting occurs during planning.
