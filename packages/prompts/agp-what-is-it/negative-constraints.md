# AGENTROPOLIS — WHAT IS IT? — NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS (binding)

Prompt-level negatives for the promo. CREATOR-owned; approved with v1.0.0.
Violation of any constraint is a QC fail and a reshoot-list item.

## Content exclusions
- No text beyond the locked copy lines. No subtitles not in the copy lock.
- No unapproved characters, mascots, or cameos.
- No 789 Studios characters, cartoon canon, or crossover imagery
  (789 <-> AGENTROPOLIS crossover is default-deny).
- No daylight, no natural outdoor scenery, no photographic live action.
- No AI watermark, provider watermark, or generation tag visible.

## Identity and continuity locks
- NEURO must keep: warm honey-brown skin, dark hair in a braided crown, red
  irises, obsidian tactical wardrobe with cyan edge trim.
- No costume change, no hair change, no eye-color change, no age shift.
- No palette drift from 60/25/15 obsidian/cyan/red.

## Text restrictions
- Locked copy must render exactly (spelling, casing, punctuation).
- No paraphrase, no added copy, no invented slogans.

## Camera restrictions
- No camera shake, no handheld, no Dutch angle, no whip pan.
- No lens flares, no bloom overload, no chromatic aberration gimmicks.

## Audio restrictions
- No music with vocals; no voice beyond the locked narration.
- No narration that paraphrases the locked copy.

## Provider restrictions
- No silent model substitution; model must be disclosed in the receipt.
- No execution without a recorded cost preview and human approval reference.

## Privacy and licensing
- No real person's likeness, no unlicensed media, no personal data.
